PRIVACY POLICY
Ring Match Sort (ReSort)
Last updated: 12 October 2026

WHO WE ARE
Mehmet Yıldırım develops Ring Match Sort. For privacy questions or data requests, contact m.yildirim9610@gmail.com.

GAME DATA ON YOUR DEVICE
The game stores progress, coins, lives, boosters, settings and reward or purchase records on your device. These records let you continue playing and prevent duplicate rewards. You do not need to create a game account. Deleting the app may remove local progress; this game does not provide its own cloud-save restoration service. Apple device backups may be managed separately through your Apple settings.

GAMEPLAY ANALYTICS
We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand gameplay and improve the game. When sharing is enabled, events can include levels, moves, wins and failures, boosters, rewards, ad outcomes and purchases. Events may contain session and attempt identifiers, reward references, and hashed purchase transaction references. These are pseudonymous identifiers, not a promise of anonymity.

Google Analytics also processes app-instance identifiers, app and device information, operating system information and usage events. Google states that Analytics uses IP addresses during collection but does not log or store them. Reports may include approximate geographic information.

Gameplay sharing is enabled by default on a new installation of this version. An existing saved choice is preserved. You can stop future gameplay analytics sharing in Settings > Privacy > STOP SHARING, and you can keep playing. ENABLE SHARING turns it on again. Turning sharing off disables Analytics collection and resets local Analytics data; it does not automatically delete data already held by Google. The setting does not disable Apple purchase processing or the separate advertising SDK.

ADS
Rewarded ads use the Google Mobile Ads SDK. This beta version requests official test ads. Ad requests can still involve network connections and technical data. Google's SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location), app/device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. We do not request permission to track you across other companies' apps or websites in this version. Firebase advertising storage and personalization settings are disabled; this does not mean that the advertising SDK collects no data.

PURCHASES
Apple processes in-app payments. The game receives product, price and transaction information needed to verify purchases, deliver coins and prevent duplicate delivery. We do not receive your full payment card details. If gameplay sharing is enabled, purchase outcomes and hashed transaction references can also be included in analytics. Apple's handling of payments and account data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

CONTACT AND SERVICE PROVIDERS
If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to send so we can respond. Do not send passwords or full payment card details. Google and Apple process information for their respective services; processing may take place outside your country. Their privacy information is available at policies.google.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy. Firebase information is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

RETENTION AND YOUR CHOICES
Local game records remain until removed with the app or device data, subject to device backup behavior. Analytics retention depends on the Google Analytics property's settings; switching sharing off is not a server-side deletion request. Apple and Google may retain service records under their own policies and applicable obligations.

You can email m.yildirim9610@gmail.com to request information, access, correction or deletion, or to object to processing where applicable. Because gameplay records use app and session identifiers rather than a game account, we may need additional information to identify records associated with your request. We cannot promise to identify every pseudonymous record from an email address alone. Requests concerning your Apple account or Google's independent processing may need to be directed to those providers. Depending on your location, you may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.

CHILDREN
The game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can assess the request and take appropriate action.

POLICY CHANGES
We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The date above identifies this version. You can read this copy without an internet connection from Settings > Privacy > PRIVACY POLICY.
