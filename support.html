ReSort: Ring Match

Support for the ring-sorting puzzle game on iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

Email m.yildirim9610@gmail.com for help, bug reports, or feedback. Include your device model, iOS version, game version, level number, and a brief description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

Game progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app may remove your progress. Contact support before reinstalling.

Coin purchases

Coin packs are optional consumable purchases processed by Apple. If a purchase does not appear, reopen the game while connected to the internet and contact support if the issue continues. For purchase history and refund requests, visit Apple purchase support.

Rewarded ads

Rewarded ads are optional and depend on internet connectivity and ad availability. An ad may not always be available. Advertising privacy choices are available in Settings > Privacy where applicable; iOS tracking permission can be changed in device Settings.

Read our Privacy Policy for information about data use and privacy choices.
